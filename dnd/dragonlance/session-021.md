# Session 21: The City Rises

**Date:** May 30, 2026

### The Ascent through the Gears of Onyari

The air grew thin and bit with an unnatural, static chill as the party crossed into the **Threshold of the Heavens**. This was no mere watchtower; it was the cerebral cortex of a flying fortress, a monument to ancient Istarian hubris. As the companions picked their way upward, their boots echoed against cold stone flanked by dizzying arrays of forgotten arcana. Massive, dead brass apparatuses hummed with dormant power, and glass-paneled consoles stood like silent sentinels—their surface runes etched specifically for monitoring weather patterns, altitude thresholds, and the structural integrity of a city suspended in the clouds.

Curiosity outpaced caution. When the party began interacting with the dormant consoles, testing the brass dials and glass interfaces, the tower reacted.

From the shadows of the vaulted ceiling, three globes of emerald fire erupted. **Flameskulls**—the chattering, insane remnants of dark mages—swung down in a synchronized arc of malice. The chamber instantly dissolved into a deafening roar of blinding heat as _fireballs_ detonated against the stone pillars. Shrapnel and arcane fire filled the room, but the party rallied through the smoke, matching the undead's fiery wrath with cold steel and precise counters, scattering the shattered, charred bone fragments across the platform.

### Captured Steel

The battle was tactical and tight. Amidst the flashing blades and exploding draconian corpses, the party’s arcanists managed a brilliant stroke of technomancy—hacking into the command runes of the constructs and **commandeering two of the Istarian drones** to fight on their side. With their newly acquired mechanical vanguard leading the charge, they pushed past a network of humming, crystalline columns that violently erupted with lethal arcs of chain lightning at anything that drew near.

### The Skies Partitioned

Near the apex of the spire, the party finally cornered **Lohezet**, the enigmatic mage of the Dragon Armies, convincing him to let them pass. But the true architect of their peril awaited them at the very absolute pinnacle of the Threshold.

There stood **Belephaion**.

As the party breached the open-air summit, the sky behind him dark with gathering storm clouds, the priest sneered. The illusion of humanity melted away. Bone shifted, flesh tore into massive iridescent scales, and wings unfurled with a terrifying crack as Belephaion transformed into his true, terrifying form: a massive **Blue Dragon**, crackling with internal lightning.

> "I will send your souls to the Dragon Queen," the dragon boomed.

Right on cue, a deep, cataclysmic shudder groaned through the foundations of the tower. Beneath the party's boots, the stones violently shifted and lurched. A deafening mechanical roar bellowed from the depths of the earth below—the irreversible launch sequence had been initiated. Onyari was taking off.

Amidst the violent tilting of the arena and the howling wind of a rising altitude, the party fought for their lives against the dragon. Dodging sweeps of a massive tail and the blinding line of his lightning breath, the companions used every ounce of their remaining strength, their commandeered drones, and tactical positioning to bring the beast down. With a final, coordinated strike, Belephaion was slain, his massive form crashing heavily onto the shaking dais as the city continued its unstoppable ascent into the upper atmosphere.
