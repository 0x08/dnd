# Session 22: The Traitor's Gate

**Date:** September 12, 2026

### The Fall of Onyari

Belephaion's corpse had barely settled on the dais before the tower began to come apart. The stone lurched beneath your boots, pillars sheared away into open air, and the Threshold of the Heavens groaned like a dying thing as the ancient mechanisms below tore themselves to pieces. There was no debate. The party turned and ran.

Bursting out into the open, you saw the true shape of what had been unleashed. A single vast fragment of the city was climbing free of the wreckage—an ancient temple of the dark gods riding a column of pale, unnatural flame into the sky. The **Bastion of Takhisis** had risen. Circling it in slow, proprietary arcs came a figure that needed no introduction: **Lord Soth**, mounted on a skeletal dragon, surveying his handiwork as the ruin of Onyari fell away beneath him.

For a few reckless moments the party tried to reach it, searching for any path, any ledge, any way up onto the rising citadel. It was hopeless. The whole city was collapsing around you—streets folding into the canyon, towers dropping out of the sky in pieces—and the choice narrowed to one: get clear, or go down with it.

### The Muster in the East

Once clear of the falling city, the party broke out the **Fargab** and raised **Darrett Highwater**. The surviving Kalaman forces had pulled back and mustered to the east, and Darrett directed you straight to them.

He listened to your report in grim silence. A flying fortress, kept aloft by cataclysmic fire, with a death knight riding escort—Darrett was certain this was a weapon unlike anything the Dragon Army had fielded before, and equally certain of where it was going. Kalaman had no idea what was coming. He urged the party to make all possible haste back to the city and raise the alarm before the Bastion arrived to do it for them.

### Passage to Kalaman

The party pressed further east without delay, to the coast, where a ship stood waiting to carry you home. The crossing brought you back to Kalaman ahead of the thing in the sky—but not by much.

### The City Forewarned

The city leadership received your account with open disbelief at first. A temple flying under its own power, crewed by the dead, was not a report anyone wanted to hear. But the party had earned its credibility a dozen times over, and the council came around: the warning was believed, and Kalaman began to brace.

The companions threw themselves into the preparations, shoring up the defences wherever hands were needed. **Dave** in particular worked tirelessly among the civilian population, moving as many refugees as could be reached out of the path of what was coming.

### The Traitor's Gate

In the middle of the preparations, an alarm went up: the **Eastern Gate** was standing unguarded.

The party moved to investigate and found the answer waiting for them at the gatehouse. **Lord Bakaris** had turned his coat. The man whose arrogance had jeopardised the city's defence a dozen times was now working the mechanisms himself, opening Kalaman's eastern approach to let Dragon Army troops walk straight in.

Justice was swift and required no deliberation. Searching the body afterward, the party turned up a note that made the betrayal worse than one man's cowardice—Bakaris's **son** had defected as well.

### A Way Below

The gate was barely secured when a **dragonnel** came down out of the sky bearing two riders: **Clystran** and Darrett.

They had flown a wide reconnaissance circuit around the Bastion of Takhisis and come back with something valuable. The underside of the citadel is not solid rock—there is a system of tunnels and catacombs running up into its foundations, and, as far as they could tell, nobody is watching them.

Their proposal was audacious. Capture more dragonnels, enough to carry the whole party, then fly up beneath the citadel and put the companions into the catacombs from below. From there, find whatever is keeping that mountain in the air—and bring it down.
