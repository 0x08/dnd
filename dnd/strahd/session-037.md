# Session 37: The Witch of the Swamp

**Date:** May 6, 2026

The infiltration quickly turned into a deadly trap; Baba Lysaga was not caught off guard, but had been hiding magically in the surrounding swamp, waiting for the characters to step inside her threshold. The moment the party entered the structure, the ancient witch surged out from the briars and clambered into a massive, floating giant's skull. Flying rapidly up into the storm clouds, she cackled loudly and unleashed the full fury of her high-level arcane repertoire upon the group.

The hut soon came to life and a root forced the door shut. Baby Lysaga unleashed a horrible cloud of poison in the cabin and everybody began to choke. Luckily the door was forced open by Kaz and for a short moment, everybody could catch their breath.

After that the battlefield became an absolute nightmare of high-tier magic. Baba Lysaga rained down devastating spells, rocking the party with *power word stun* and a lethal green ray of *finger of death*. Simultaneously, the ground churned as she summoned massive swarms of biting insects.

A strange green glow was seen coming from the bottom of the hut and after multiple attempts, the party managed to rip out the floorboards and remove the green gem that was powering the hut itself. As soon as it was removed the hut returned to normal; this futher enraged Baba Lysaga who doubled down on her effort to kill the party.

In a rather spectacular move, Ulvor was able to climb up onto the hut and then jumped onto Lysaga's skull, dealing her a fatal blow as her skull fell to the ground.
