# Session 38: Return to the Vineyards

**Date:** June 10, 2026

### The Spoils of Berez

With the deathly silence of the ruins of Berez settling around them, the party thoroughly ransacked the creeping, animated remains of Baba Lysaga’s hut. Beneath the rot and dark arcana of the fallen witch, they located her iron-bound chest, breaking its seals to claim the wealth and strange relics hidden within.

Yet, the true prize was the glowing, pulsing green gemstone—the stolen soul of the vineyards.

Wasting no time, the companions traveled north through the choking mists, navigating the treacherous Svalich Woods until the silhouette of the **Wizard of Wines** winery broke through the fog. **Davian Martikov** met them at the threshold, his weathered face breaking into a rare, tearful expression of profound relief upon seeing the second magical gem restored to safety.

Without delaying, the party accompanied the old wereraven out into the blighted, dying fields. **Gillian** took the lead, wielding a shovel to cut deep into the damp Barovian soil at the heart of the roots. Together, they buried the vibrant artifact deep beneath the vineyard's core. Almost instantly, a subtle, warm hum radiated outward through the earth, sending a spark of revitalizing life back into the withered vines.

Overwhelmed with gratitude, Davian offered the party the absolute best of his remaining cellars, pouring generous draughts of Purple Grapemash No. 3. For the first time in weeks, the party enjoyed a night of safety, warmth, and heavy sleep within the reinforced stone walls of the winery.

### The Werewolf Den

The morning brought cold reality back into focus. Refleshed and provisioned, the party bid farewell to the Martikovs and set off toward the dense, untamed forest sprawling to the west of Van Richten’s lonely tower. Their objective: track down the elusive and predatory **Werewolf Den**.

The trek was grueling and disorienting. The western woods were thick with ancient brambles and unnatural briars that seemed to actively snatch at clothes and armor. Hours melted into a monotonous blur of gray trees and damp moss. However, by relying on keen wilderness tracking and following a scent of iron and wet fur, the party eventually broke through the tree line onto the jagged, mist-crowned shores of **Lake Baratok**.

There, carved into the sheer face of the rocky cliffs, hung their destination.

The cave entrance was an imposing, terrifying monument of natural stone and ancient carvings, perfectly shaped to mirror a **gargantuan, snarling wolf's mouth**. The jagged stalactites and stalagmites formed rows of razor-sharp teeth, framing a dark, gaping gullet that exhaled the hot, musk-laden stench of apex predators.
